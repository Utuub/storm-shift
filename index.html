It's the closing shift at Corner Fresh Market. The last customer left twenty minutes ago and you're mopping aisle 4 when the tornado sirens go off.

Dale, the general manager, locks the front doors and tells everyone to get away from the windows.

Then the storm hits the building.

When it passes, nothing is broken, but every one of the store's 1,500 items is on the floor. Dale has one rule: nobody clocks out until this store is back in order.

Put every item back in its own spot. Each one earns store credit, and Dale will trade credit for upgrades at the office desk.
